## 1. First principles: why "once, permanently, anything on a computer" cannot follow

Let `H` be the hypothesis space the system can construct, `I` the invariances it already holds, `O` the observations it can execute, and `V` the independent verdict that reads them. A one-shot trace `t` can become permanent **storage**; it becomes capability only when all of the following hold:

```
∃ h* ∈ H(I, failure_evidence)          the truth is representable
∧ ∃ o ∈ O  : o(h*) ≠ o(h′)            some computable observation splits it
∧ V(o, h*) is independent/affordable    the split is actually readable
```

The same conclusion is one line of information arithmetic:

```
bits_after = log2(|H| / |G|) − I(E) − Σ I(q) − I(interface)
```

`I(E)` is one demonstration, `I(q)` is one chosen question, and the authored space/prior `I(interface)` is the only term not billed per interaction. Here the example does not change between **10%** and **100%** leader accuracy — the space does. Learning finishes only at zero bits, not after a fixed number of experiences. If any factor is zero, more demonstrations, more environments, more compute, and more "advanced actions" multiply by zero. This is not an opinion: in 23 real program spaces one demonstration leaves a median **1,037** survivors (**10.018 bits**); on **479** real sites one success licenses **0** requirements and would act wrongly on **297 / 319** held-out sites (precision **0.069**); two *chosen* observations reach precision **1.000** with **0** dangerous errors; **12** random sequences identify **0**; **7 / 23** tasks with no repair in the space were unrescuable by any number of questions. Rice-style undecidability and finite-evaluation Goodhart limits mean no fixed verifier can certify arbitrary semantic intent from a passing trace [1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.07663v1), [2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.24206), [3](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.11464v1).

A demonstrated action is also **an operation plus a premise**, not a bare command. On four real machines, **5 / 18** atoms change effect between `TARGET_ABSENT` and `TARGET_EXISTS`, **17 / 36 (47.2%)** cells are inert, and **4** atoms ignore their argument entirely. The correct unit is `(atom × state class)`, not "action". A single trace is one cell of that table. The final replay audit closes the other half of "replay forever": over **228** ordered two-step compositions the operation stayed legal **228 / 228** times, but **122 / 228** recorded plans were stale and **71 / 228** silently corrupted a file that still parsed. A plan is a function of the state, not a permanent macro; only a proof run on the state in hand licenses acting on that state. The closed `git` basin makes the same point without GUI uncertainty: **168** reachable semantic states and **1,680** real transitions were enumerated; the best three-action demonstration reaches **112 / 168** states for a status-level goal and **56 / 168** for a goal with an unmentioned premise. Adding the file-content sense, not another demonstration, changes the premise task from **24** undecidable states to **0**; command-output-only feedback leaves **168 / 168** undecidable.

**"Best part is no part" is the engineering law applied here.** The tree already owns the constructive, interventional, identification, admission, planning, composition, environment-learning, and proof-obligation organs under non-obvious names. `WHERE_THE_SOLUTIONS_LIVE_20261101_V1.md` says the quiet truth: *"The decision layer is not missing. It is unwired from the computer-use layer."* Rebuilding a learner, verifier, or weight house would add a part that the measurements say cannot supply `H`, `I`, `O`, or `V`.

## 4. The necessary condition, and why there is no single lever

The standing answer was **separation**. The newest measurements keep it as a *necessary condition* but falsify the owner's **"single bottleneck that unlocks the most capability"** premise on the measured browser suite:

> **Capability = (a hypothesis space that contains the truth) × (an invariance set that gives one demonstration reach) × (a causal observation that splits the survivors) × (an independent verifier that can read the split).**
>
> This is a conjunction of necessary conditions, not a claim that one fixed part supplies all four. The measured remainder is a long tail of missing factors.

- **Size of `H` = vocabulary.** Three composed vocabularies reach **24 / 40** and stop; **16** tasks remain hard-open. No fixed vocabulary covers them. A fourth hand-written family, more search, or the hidden CEGIS instrument (0 / 5 calibration, 0 / 3 open) was measured dead.
- **“Once” is arithmetic, and the prior is not learned from the trace.** `bits_after = log2(|H|/|G|) − I(E) − Σ I(q) − I(interface)`; the authoring of `H` and `I(interface)` is the only term not billed per interaction. A chosen question beats another demonstration **1.747×** in this measured space; choosing a demonstration by how many distinct answers it yields is refuted (**2 / 37** rank agreement). This is why writing down the hypothesis space before spending an interaction is smarter than adding another experience, and why the ratio is a measurement of this space rather than a universal constant.
- **Reach per demonstration = invariant set `I`.** Literal preconditions fire 304 times and win 0; α-canonical preconditions fire 384 times and win 4, but all 4 are already-repaired tasks. One invariance multiplies reach, not vocabulary.
- **Readability of the split = `O` + `V`.** The operation proof (~55 ms) decides 18 static-observable-identical sites; the corpus `pytest` is the only repair judge; a fast screen lied on 3 no-JSON candidates; majority vote among test-passing candidates is not identification; self-evolved verifiers collapse without an anchored outer reference [3](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.11464v1). The final replay audit adds that a stored plan needs a fresh proof on the current state: legality survived **228 / 228** changes, while **122 / 228** plans were stale and **71 / 228** silently corrupted a still-parsing file.
- **The affirmative half is measured, not a lever.** Proof-checked composition reaches **31 / 80** two-step attempts with **0** damaged worlds, but leaves **34** without a second candidate and **22** dead-premise refusals. It is one factor of the conjunction, not the single unlock; the remaining halves are a proposal generator pointed at the unpassed bugs and a per-step verifier for long horizons.
- **The GUI side has no quotient to buy reach.** The code-side α-invariance lever worked because bound names were arbitrary author choices the template keyed on. GUI `id`/`name`/DOM order are either never read as terms or are the answer itself; the page judge accepts total equality. Building GUI invariance machinery would be a part with no job. The surviving boundary is an operand vocabulary that no page can show.
- **The remainder is a long tail, not a lever.** Of **46** unsolved MiniWoB++ pages, **16** instruction-need classes remain; the largest is **6** pages. A declared-format prior covers **6 / 123** usable pages and **84** pages have nothing to type. Any "the one bottleneck is X" claim is falsifiable by counting. The ranked next moves are: strip the English phrase from the operand trigger while keeping the arithmetic; add a pointer channel only for the **6** pages that need a location, on real Chromium; strengthen the verifier where code has an oracle; reduce effective horizon by hierarchy; and leave weights last, procedure-only. Each has a measured pass/kill condition.
- **Named gaps and missing operands remain local.** Reading first names **21** demonstrable gaps without a false negative; adding the missing operand wins **5** design-set pages and **0** held-out pages. Neither is "learn once, transfer anywhere": the first is a policy over existing observations, the second is a vocabulary addition with a measured transfer boundary.
- **Composition/addressing is a symptom, not a second bottleneck.** The new-environment refusals need a composer over measured `(precondition, effect)` operators. That composer already exists under non-obvious names: `capability_planner.py`, `generic_goal_tool_effect_planner_v1.py`, `capability_composer_v1.py`, `verified_skill_macro_planner_v1.py`, `explicit_recovery_planner.py`, `environment_learning_v1.py`, `predicate_world_abstraction_v1.py`, `operator_semantics_v1.py`, `hierarchical_skill_library_v1.py`. Wiring them to win the single measured `TARBALL REPLACE` refusal would be an ordinary **+1 / 16**, exactly the incremental gain the owner told us to kill. The next unit is **a measured sense or part derived from one specific failure, plus a fresh proof that its premise holds on the state in hand** — not another demonstration, action, environment, one-lever search, or weight.
