mypy #21968: exhaustive matching for tuple[bool, bool]

Project Brain real SWE mission ASTRA-REAL-SWE-MYPY-21968-001

Status: locally fixed and regression-verified against mypy master b645e069c1e7f907205e6cc0eabea5d2b5beeefc.

Observed defect

A four-case structural match covering all combinations of two booleans was incorrectly reported as non-exhaustive, producing both [exhaustive-match] and [return].

Root cause

For fixed-length tuple sequence patterns, mypy only applied negative narrowing when zero or one element could conditionally fail. A pattern such as (True, True) has two conditional subpatterns, so the unmatched type fell back to the original tuple[bool, bool].

Repair

Partition the complement by the first subpattern that fails. For patterns P1..Pn, represent the unmatched space as !P1 OR (P1 AND !P2) OR .... This is linear in tuple length and gives exact negative narrowing for fixed tuples.

Specification basis

Python's structural-pattern-matching specification states that fixed-length sequence subpatterns are matched left-to-right and matching stops on the first failing subpattern. This directly supports the complement partition used by the repair.

Sources: https://peps.python.org/pep-0634/ and https://docs.python.org/3/reference/compound_stmts.html#the-match-statement

Additional precision improvement

For tuple[Literal[1,2], Literal["a","b"]] after matching (1,"a"), mypy now preserves the exact remainder: tuple[Literal[2], Literal["a"] | Literal["b"]] | tuple[Literal[1], Literal["b"]], instead of retaining a broader superset that still included the matched case.
