## Absolute producer-path rule

A Brain-credited result MUST NOT depend on another model for producer cognition.

Producer cognition includes reasoning, planning, decomposition, synthesis, interpretation, policy selection, tool selection, code or answer generation, self-critique, recovery decisions, judgment, or any other transformation that materially supplies the target problem-solving behavior.

The prohibition is identity- and hosting-independent. It applies equally to remote model APIs, local models, open-weight models, copied or vendored weights, self-hosted model servers, cached model artifacts, renamed or wrapped models, and models invoked through runtimes such as llama.cpp, vLLM, Ollama, transformers, LiteLLM, or equivalents.

Local possession, a fixed hash, zero monetary cost, open weights, or a Brain-authored wrapper does not turn another model's cognition into Brain cognition.

## Credit consequence

If any prohibited model contributes producer cognition:
- Brain capability credit = 0
- Brain benchmark credit = 0
- Brain acceptance credit = 0
- Brain ownership credit = 0
- Brain promotion credit = 0
- Brain terminal credit = 0

The run may be retained only as contaminated diagnostic/specimen evidence and MUST be attributed as model-assisted, never as "Brain passed".

Unknown or incomplete cognition provenance fails closed exactly like a detected model dependency.

## Allowed external roles

External models may be used only outside the credited producer path, for specimen/target observation, comparator, independently separated evaluator when required by the benchmark, or external knowledge source whose output is treated as untrusted information rather than target-solving policy. Such roles confer no Brain capability ownership.

## Brain-owned learned artifacts

A narrow learned artifact may count as a Brain-owned capability mechanism only after it is itself the operative Brain artifact, passes donor-deletion and full dependency accounting, and does not rely on a separable external/general-purpose model to provide the target cognition at runtime.

A general-purpose pretrained model acting as reasoner/planner/agent is not made admissible by storing its weights inside Brain.

## No laundering

Forbidden equivalences include:
- self-hosted model cognition == Brain cognition
- open-weight model cognition == Brain cognition
- model plus Brain prompt/router == Brain-owned target capability
- content-addressed model weights == Brain-owned capability
- model-assisted benchmark success == Brain benchmark success
- model-assisted task success == proof Brain possesses the reasoning used to obtain it

Any load-bearing contaminated subresult contaminates the parent claim.

## Permanence

This rule may not be weakened by optimization, benchmark pressure, provider changes, new model names, architectural relabeling, or ordinary inferred supersession.

Only a future explicit owner instruction that directly names this prohibition and explicitly rescinds it may change this rule. Silence, convenience, or a newer theory cannot.
