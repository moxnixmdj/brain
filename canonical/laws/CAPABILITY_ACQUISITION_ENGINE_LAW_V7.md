## Purpose

Acquire existing Opus-5.5-or-better capabilities and internalize their reusable operative configuration into Brain by the fastest sufficient route, including causal extraction from white-box donors when simpler routes do not suffice.

## Progress states

1. UNMAPPED
2. EXTERNAL_CAPABILITY_SPECIMEN_FOUND
3. REUSABLE_MECHANISM_IDENTIFIED
4. WHITE_BOX_DONOR_IDENTIFIED
5. HARVESTED_CAPABILITY_CANDIDATE
6. INTERNALIZATION_IN_PROGRESS
7. BRAIN_CONFIGURED_VERIFICATION_PENDING
8. VERIFIED_OWNED_EQUAL_OR_BETTER
9. REOPENED_BY_REGRESSION
10. UNRESOLVED_THEORY_FALSIFICATION_CANDIDATE

Finding a better model advances at most to EXTERNAL_CAPABILITY_SPECIMEN_FOUND.
Finding inspectable weights advances at most to WHITE_BOX_DONOR_IDENTIFIED.
Neither is ownership.

## Promotion law

OWNED credit requires:
- target useful behavior and frontier bar defined independently of donor identity;
- solved/JIT/deterministic functions removed from the residual contract;
- an already-existing reusable mechanism or lawful white-box donor identified;
- operative configuration represented in Brain-owned artifacts;
- no opaque external hidden cognition credited as the target capability;
- future invocation requires no rediscovery or re-extraction;
- when harvesting is used, causal ablation/rescue, specificity, held-out generalization, and donor-deletion tests pass;
- the exact Brain-configured route is tested;
- result >= corresponding Opus 5.5 bar;
- durable capability package/capsule saved.

## General computation substrate

Models may execute Brain-owned capability configurations.

A model is a substrate when Brain supplies the operative capability structure.

A donor or model is an external capability provider when Brain's route reduces to "ask this model to do C because it already knows how."

That receives no ownership credit.

A harvested capsule may depend on a disclosed Brain-owned base substrate, but the complete unavoidable runtime dependency footprint must be counted.

## Acquisition order

For every target:
1. already-owned Brain mechanisms;
2. delete capability demand that is already unnecessary;
3. deterministic/software/algorithmic substitutes;
4. exposed skills/procedures/prompts/workflows;
5. open-source agent scaffolds and capability libraries;
6. published/exposed reusable methods and compositions;
7. white-box capability harvesting from admissible donors;
8. configuration/integration of the winning existing mechanism;
9. external opaque systems only as specimens/evidence sources.

Do not stop at a benchmark-winning model.
Do not harvest neural machinery when a simpler verified substitute already closes the gap.

## White-box harvesting

White-box harvesting is acquisition of an already-existing learned mechanism, not invention.

It is governed by:
- canonical/laws/WHITE_BOX_CAPABILITY_HARVESTING_LAW_V1.md
- canonical/governance/WHITE_BOX_CAPABILITY_CAPSULE_SCHEMA_V1.json

## Build boundary

Creating glue, adapters, extraction/minimization tooling, packaging, routing, verification, and recovery around an already-existing mechanism is allowed.

Inventing the underlying useful capability from first principles is forbidden.

## Verification

Verification must exercise the exact Brain-internalized route.

Donor/specimen evidence is discovery and causal-localization evidence only.

Untouched qualification tasks remain quarantined until final qualification.

## Priority

1. correct internalized capability >= Opus 5.5;
2. permanent Brain ownership and donor independence;
3. minimum wall-clock to internalization;
4. minimum total durable resource cost;
5. $0 incremental spend;
6. minimum complexity.
