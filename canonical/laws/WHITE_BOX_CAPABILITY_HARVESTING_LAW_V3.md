## Donor role

A donor is acquisition-time evidence and causal source only. It is never a credited runtime cognition provider.

Allowed transfer mechanisms include distillation, imitation, synthetic-data teaching, fine-tuning of Brain-owned substrate, sparse/low-rank transfer, task/checkpoint deltas, module/expert/circuit extraction, algorithm/procedure recovery, mechanistic reimplementation, and verified composition.

A method name does not grant ownership.

## Mandatory transfer-vs-outsourcing gate

Every donor-derived promotion must pass:
- canonical/runtime/capability_transfer_ownership_gate_v1.py

The exact transfer record must prove:
- acquisition/deployment phase separation;
- donor use confined to acquisition;
- exact Brain-owned derived operative artifact;
- donor runtime dependency count = 0;
- external-model runtime dependency count = 0;
- online teacher calls = 0;
- teacher fallback paths = 0;
- donor unavailable during qualification;
- post-deletion capability preservation;
- untouched held-out qualification;
- causal ablation and rescue;
- complete runtime dependency inventory;
- no answer leakage;
- no future donor rediscovery requirement;
- derived artifact distinct from the donor.

Any unknown field fails closed.

## Distillation

Distillation is legitimate internalization only when the student is the operative Brain mechanism after the teacher is removed.

Teacher inference, teacher fallback, teacher critique, teacher reranking, teacher hidden-state access, teacher online planning, or a copied/rehosted teacher are cognition outsourcing and receive zero Brain credit.

## Donor deletion

Donor deletion is literal with respect to target-solving cognition. Qualification must be performed after the donor is unavailable to the producer path.

No earlier permission for an "allowed general substrate" may be interpreted to permit another general-purpose model to supply credited cognition.

## Promotion

A donor-derived artifact cannot reach VERIFIED_OWNED_EQUAL_OR_BETTER unless:
1. the transfer gate passes;
2. the capability capsule is complete;
3. donor deletion passes;
4. independent held-out qualification passes;
5. exact dependency closure is verified;
6. the future runtime contains no external/general-purpose cognition donor.

Failure of any one condition gives zero ownership credit.
