## Mission

Make the Astra capability surface callable and operational in Project Brain as fast as physically possible, using anything legitimate that already exists.

Workers, Luna, Sol, Brain, GDI, benchmarks, predicates, runtimes, and superhero identities are means only.

## Objective

Astra is an AND-gated target.

Let T_i be wall-clock time until required capability family i is operational and verified in the common runtime, and T_integration the time until the combined runtime works end-to-end.

Optimize:

min max(T_1, T_2, ..., T_n, T_integration)

Do not maximize total activity.
Do not optimize average family progress.
Do not serialize families unless dependencies require it.
Allocate capacity to minimize time-to-all-required-capabilities.

## Program: Astra Closure Race

For every required capability family simultaneously maintain one closure record with:
- strongest implementation already inside Project Brain;
- strongest legitimate $0 external implementation available now;
- exact residual behavior gap;
- fastest plausible acquisition route;
- competing fallback routes;
- integration interface;
- falsification/effect test;
- estimated wall-clock time to closure;
- workers currently attacking closure;
- blocker age and latest evidence.

The slowest credible finishing family receives disproportionate parallel force.

## Four-route race per family

Run these routes in parallel when useful:

A. DIRECT REUSE
Find an existing system that already satisfies the capability and expose it through ASTRA_RUNTIME.

B. COMPOSITION
Combine models, tools, external/JIT knowledge, solvers, programs, and deterministic software to obtain the behavior.

C. ADAPTATION
Modify the smallest seam of an existing implementation.

D. INVENTION
Only after A-C are falsified or clearly slower under the mission constraints.

First route that survives real effect verification wins. Stop losing routes.

## One continuously evolving ASTRA_RUNTIME

Do not build ten isolated capability projects.

All winning routes are integrated immediately behind one common ASTRA_RUNTIME with:
- goal/requirements compiler;
- capability/skill router;
- knowledge router;
- tools/actions;
- durable Brain state;
- verification/falsification;
- recovery/checkpointing;
- fallback suppliers.

Integration happens continuously because integration failures change closure estimates.

## Real-mission pressure loop

As soon as plausible implementations exist, run fresh consequential cross-domain missions through ASTRA_RUNTIME.

For every observed failure:
1. isolate the exact residual missing behavior;
2. launch a JIT acquisition race for that residual;
3. use workers to search, reproduce, compare, adapt, integrate, and falsify;
4. bind the winning route;
5. replay the failed action immediately;
6. resume the same mission;
7. inherit the verified repair population-wide.

Real missions expose composition failures. They do not replace the target definition.

## Worker population role

Luna/cheap workers are the massive procurement/integration workforce:
- search;
- code;
- test;
- reproduce;
- compare;
- adapt;
- integrate;
- falsify;
- discover more legitimate capacity.

Sol-class or stronger cognition is used at high-multiplication decisions:
- decomposition;
- route selection;
- ambiguous diagnosis;
- synthesis;
- promotion.

Deterministic computation handles work that does not require language-model intelligence.

## Capacity expansion

Worker-capacity expansion runs in parallel and is never a prerequisite to capability construction.

Every real $0 worker available now attacks closure now.
A subset simultaneously searches for more legitimate execution/inference capacity.
New capacity joins immediately.
Do not stop Astra construction to build a worker factory.

The real missing substrate is useful model turns / execution capacity, not registered identities.

The rate of acquiring capabilities should increase while capabilities are being acquired.
