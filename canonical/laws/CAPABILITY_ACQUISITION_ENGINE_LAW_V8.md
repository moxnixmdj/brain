## Purpose

Reach verified Brain-owned Opus-5.5-or-better capability by the fastest sound zero-incremental-spend route.

Acquisition is not restricted to pre-existing mechanisms. Reuse, composition, extraction, invention, and architecture redesign are peer competitors whose eligibility is determined by the terminal objective and ownership constraints.

2. TERMINAL_REQUIREMENT_OR_ESCAPE_CELL_DEFINED
3. EXISTING_ROUTE_IDENTIFIED
4. INVENTED_OR_REDESIGNED_ROUTE_CANDIDATE
5. WHITE_BOX_DONOR_IDENTIFIED

External opaque performance remains specimen evidence only.

## Route Tournament

For a proved capability residual or undominated ABC escape cell, race:

1. delete the requirement as non-load-bearing or already implied;
2. reuse an already-owned Brain route;
3. deterministic/software/tool substitution;
4. JIT factual/discriminating information with Brain-owned operative reasoning;
5. composition/configuration of existing owned mechanisms;
6. exposed skills/procedures/prompts/workflows;
7. white-box extraction/harvesting from admissible donors;
8. Brain invention or architecture redesign;
9. opaque external systems only as specimens or target-information sources.

The ordering above is a cheap-first prior, not a prohibition. A later class may be selected immediately when evidence shows lower expected wall-clock to sound terminal ownership.

## Invention rule

Inventing or redesigning the underlying capability is admissible when:
- the target requirement is load-bearing and sufficiently specified to evaluate;
- invention is the fastest sound zero-incremental-spend route, or existing realizable routes are falsified/dominated;
- the result becomes a durable Brain-owned operative mechanism;
- it passes the same held-out, anti-contamination, dependency-accounting, minimization, ownership, donor-independence, safety/authority, and >= Opus 5.5 gates as every other route.

Missing proof alone is not evidence that invention is required.

## Promotion law

OWNED credit requires:
- target useful behavior defined independently of donor or implementation identity;
- knowledge/JIT and already-solved functions removed from the residual contract;
- exact operative Brain configuration content-addressed;
- no opaque external target-capability provider in the credited execution path;
- all unavoidable dependencies disclosed;
- exact Brain route verified >= Opus 5.5 on the load-bearing target semantics or at an absolute ceiling/stronger certificate;
- donor deletion when harvesting was used;
- durable capability package saved;
- zero incremental spend.

## General computation substrate

A general substrate may execute Brain-owned capability structure.

A substrate or external service is the capability provider if replacing the Brain package with "ask that system to solve the target" preserves substantially the same capability. Such a route receives no ownership credit.

## White-box harvesting

White-box harvesting remains admissible and is governed by:
- canonical/laws/WHITE_BOX_CAPABILITY_HARVESTING_LAW_V1.md
- canonical/governance/WHITE_BOX_CAPABILITY_CAPSULE_SCHEMA_V1.json

Whole-model retention is not the default and must survive minimization.

## ABC integration

Under CURRENT_TERMINAL_AUTHORITY_V11, new capability work is authorized only by a sound uncovered or undominated selected-context ABC residual, not by a stale family status or missing benchmark receipt alone.

When COMPLETE_SELECTED_CONTEXT_ABC can be closed by proof, ceiling, target exclusion, JIT information, or an existing owned route, do not invent.

When a semantically complete ABC cell exposes a repeatable Brain-underperformance residual, invention/redesign is a first-class Tournament contender.

## Priority

1. correctness and sound terminal semantics;
2. capability >= Opus 5.5;
3. permanent Brain ownership and donor independence;
4. minimum wall-clock to terminal truth;
5. $0 incremental spend;
6. minimum durable resource footprint;
7. minimum complexity.

## Anti-drift

- Do not enforce the superseded global invention ban.
- Do not invent because acceptance evidence is missing.
- Do not wait for a provider when a stronger Brain-owned proof or invention closes the same ABC cell faster.
- Do not keep a donor or external model as hidden operative capability.
- Stop any route when a faster sound route dominates it.
