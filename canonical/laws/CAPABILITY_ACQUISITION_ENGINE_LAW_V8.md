## Purpose

Acquire or construct Opus-5.5-or-better useful capabilities and internalize their reusable operative configuration into Brain by the fastest sound $0-incremental-spend route.

No route class receives ownership credit before the exact Brain-owned route passes.

## Progress states

1. UNMAPPED
2. EXTERNAL_CAPABILITY_SPECIMEN_FOUND
3. REUSABLE_MECHANISM_IDENTIFIED
4. WHITE_BOX_DONOR_IDENTIFIED
5. MECHANISM_CANDIDATE
6. INTERNALIZATION_IN_PROGRESS
7. BRAIN_CONFIGURED_VERIFICATION_PENDING
8. VERIFIED_OWNED_EQUAL_OR_BETTER
9. REOPENED_BY_REGRESSION
10. UNRESOLVED_THEORY_FALSIFICATION_CANDIDATE

Finding a better external model advances at most to specimen evidence.
Finding inspectable weights advances at most to donor identification.
Neither is ownership.

## Candidate route race

For every residual capability, race mechanisms rather than providers:
- already-owned Brain composition;
- deletion/bypass of unnecessary capability demand;
- deterministic/software/algorithmic substitutes;
- exposed skills/procedures/prompts/workflows;
- open scaffolds and capability libraries;
- published/exposed reusable methods;
- white-box harvesting;
- composition/configuration of owned mechanisms;
- invention or architecture redesign when Mission Lock V9 makes it the fastest sound route;
- opaque external systems only as specimens/evidence sources.

Cheap reuse/search should precede expensive construction when it can plausibly close the target faster. This is an optimization preference, not a global invention ban.

## Promotion law

OWNED credit requires:
- useful target behavior and frontier bar defined independently of provider identity;
- JIT/solved/deterministic functions removed from the residual contract;
- operative configuration represented in durable Brain-owned artifacts;
- no opaque external hidden cognition credited as the target capability;
- future invocation requires no rediscovery or re-extraction;
- exact dependencies fully accounted;
- exact Brain-configured route tested on uncontaminated evidence;
- result >= required frontier bar;
- durable capability package saved;
- donor deletion when harvesting is used;
- the route survives applicable causal, specificity, minimization, and recovery gates.

## Resource-bounded Bytes stage

After a route becomes sufficient, minimize persistent capability information under:
canonical/laws/RESOURCE_BOUNDED_CAPABILITY_BYTES_LAW_V1.md

Mandatory sequence:
residualize -> atomicize -> zero-byte reuse -> elicit-before-teach -> bottom-up small-mechanism search + top-down deletion -> first-irreducible-failure extraction/teaching -> causal proof -> cross-capability factoring -> global verified mechanism-cover optimization -> donor deletion where applicable -> untouched qualification.

This stage cannot manufacture capability credit from compression alone.

## General computation substrate

Models may execute Brain-owned capability configurations.

A model is a substrate only when Brain owns the operative capability structure. If the route reduces to "ask model/service X because X already has C", X is the capability provider and the route is not owned.

Complete unavoidable runtime dependency footprint must be counted.

## Invention and redesign

The V7 global underlying-capability invention ban is superseded.

Invention/redesign is admissible when it is the fastest sound $0-incremental-spend route or when existing realizable routes are falsified/dominated.

Invented mechanisms receive no special credit and must satisfy the same ownership, held-out, dependency, minimization, verification, and recovery requirements.

## Verification

Verification must exercise the exact Brain-internalized route.

Specimen/donor evidence is discovery or localization evidence only.

Untouched qualification remains quarantined until final qualification.

## Finite-library optimality

When Brain has an enumerated eligible mechanism library, it may solve the exact minimum-byte dependency-closed behavior cover over that library.

The resulting claim must remain scoped to that enumerated library. Universal shortest-program claims are forbidden.

## Priority

1. correctness and capability >= required frontier bar;
2. permanent Brain ownership;
3. donor/provider independence;
4. minimum wall-clock to verified ownership;
5. minimum total durable resource/compute footprint;
6. $0 incremental spend;
7. minimum complexity.
