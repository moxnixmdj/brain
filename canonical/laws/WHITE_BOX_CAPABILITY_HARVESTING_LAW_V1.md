# WHITE-BOX CAPABILITY HARVESTING LAW V1

Status: CANONICAL / MANDATORY / FAIL-CLOSED

## Purpose

Acquire already-existing learned capability mechanisms from legally usable open-weight or otherwise inspectable donors without adopting the donor as Brain, importing redundant knowledge, or retaining the donor as an opaque runtime capability provider.

## Core rule

A donor is a temporary causal source.

The target artifact is the smallest sufficient Brain-owned capability capsule.

Donor performance alone gives zero ownership credit.

## Neural necessity mask

Before harvesting, remove from the target contract everything already solved by:
- Brain-owned deterministic software or algorithms;
- exact symbolic computation;
- external/JIT knowledge retrieval;
- durable Brain memory/state;
- tools, browser, computer-use surfaces, and artifact engines;
- already-owned capability packages;
- cheaper verified substitutes.

Only the residual learned behavior may justify neural harvesting.

## Donor admissibility

A donor is admissible only when:
- exact model/checkpoint/version is identified;
- weights or other operative internals are inspectable enough for the selected extraction method;
- license and use/derivative constraints are recorded and compatible with the intended Brain artifact;
- provenance and hashes are retained;
- donor access does not require incremental spend above the mission limit;
- the donor is not treated as the promoted runtime implementation.

When redistribution rights are unclear, retain only lawful metadata/derivative artifacts and fail closed on redistribution.

## Extraction classes

Allowed classes include:
- same-lineage checkpoint or task-vector deltas;
- sparse or low-rank weight deltas/adapters;
- MoE expert/module subsets;
- layer/head/circuit subsets when causally demonstrated;
- compact distilled residual models/modules;
- explicit algorithms or procedures recovered from donor behavior;
- compositions of the above.

Activation features, probes, SAEs, attribution maps, and correlations are diagnostic evidence only unless converted into a donor-independent operative mechanism that passes all promotion tests.

## Causal sufficiency tests

Before a harvested artifact may be promoted:

1. TARGET CONTRACT
   Define the residual useful behavior independently of donor identity.

2. DONOR POSITIVE CONTROL
   The donor or donor-derived reference route demonstrates the target behavior under the chosen evaluation.

3. ABLATION
   Removing or disabling the proposed mechanism causes a material target-capability loss under matched conditions.

4. RESCUE
   Restoring/transplanting the mechanism restores the target capability under matched conditions.

5. SPECIFICITY
   The artifact does not obtain the score only by leaking answers, importing unrelated knowledge, or destroying important control capabilities.

6. HELD-OUT GENERALIZATION
   Final capability evidence uses tasks/examples not used to select, train, prune, rank, or tune the capsule.

7. DONOR DELETION
   The promoted Brain route runs without the donor model/checkpoint/service supplying the target capability at runtime.

8. BRAIN ROUTE VERIFICATION
   The exact Brain configuration that future work will invoke independently verifies at or above the corresponding Opus 5.5 useful-capability bar.

Failure of any test means no full ownership credit.

## Capability capsule

Every promoted harvested capability must conform to:
- canonical/governance/WHITE_BOX_CAPABILITY_CAPSULE_SCHEMA_V1.json

The capsule must include exact operative artifacts, unavoidable dependencies, license/provenance, causal evidence, held-out evidence, interference controls, invocation contract, minimum runtime resources, hashes, verifier, recovery/fallback, and donor-deletion proof.

A nominally small adapter whose runtime requires a giant donor/base model must disclose the full unavoidable dependency footprint. Hidden dependency parameters do not disappear because humans rename them "base model."

## Minimization

After causal sufficiency is established, minimize the capsule by deleting unnecessary:
- experts;
- ranks;
- layers;
- heads;
- precision;
- prompt scaffolding;
- context requirements;
- auxiliary learned modules.

After every deletion, rerun target and control verification.

Optimization target:
minimum durable resource cost subject to capability >= required bar.

Parameter count is a diagnostic, not the terminal metric.

## Contamination

Untouched qualification tasks may not be read, trained on, tuned against, donor-selected against, or otherwise used before final qualification.

Spent tasks and explicitly designated calibration sets may be used for localization and extraction.

## Shared cognition factoring

After multiple capsules exist, search for duplicated learned substrate across them.

Shared substrate may be factored into one Brain-owned cognition kernel only when:
- each affected capsule remains re-executable;
- exact dependency accounting is preserved;
- capability and control regressions do not occur;
- the shared kernel itself is Brain-owned and versioned.

Do not assume a general neural kernel is necessary. Reality decides.

## Stop rule

Stop harvesting for a target immediately when a simpler already-owned, deterministic, software, or existing reusable route meets the target bar with lower total cost and equal or better reliability/verifiability.
