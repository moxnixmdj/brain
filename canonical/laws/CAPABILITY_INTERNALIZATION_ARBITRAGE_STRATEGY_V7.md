## Route race

Race existing capability mechanisms, not merely providers.

Candidate classes:
1. already-owned Brain capability configuration;
2. deletion/bypass of unnecessary capability demand;
3. deterministic or software substitute;
4. open skills/procedure bundles;
5. agent scaffolds and algorithmic workflows;
6. prompt/policy/tool configurations;
7. published/exposed reusable methods;
8. white-box donor harvesting;
9. compositions/configurations of existing mechanisms;
10. opaque external models/services only as capability specimens.

## White-box donor race

For a residual learned capability, choose donors by extraction value, not model prestige.

Prefer donors that maximize:
- exact target capability strength;
- legal/technical inspectability;
- paired or related checkpoints that expose learned deltas;
- modularity, including expert/module structure;
- ability to generate causal contrasts;
- transferability to a small donor-independent capsule;
- low extraction and verification cost.

Different capability gaps may use different donors.

## Extraction race

When white-box harvesting survives OBS, race:
1. same-lineage weight/task deltas;
2. sparse/low-rank adapters or deltas;
3. expert/module/layer subsets with causal proof;
4. residual capability distillation;
5. explicit algorithm/procedure recovery.

Activation-only feature steering is diagnostic unless it produces a donor-independent operative capsule with causal proof.

## Dominance rule

A specimen can dominate discovery but cannot win ownership.

A donor can dominate extraction but cannot remain the hidden promoted target-capability provider.

The winning ownership route is the fastest route whose operative mechanism becomes Brain-owned, whose unavoidable dependencies are fully disclosed, and whose Brain execution meets/exceeds Opus 5.5.

## No-whole-model-default rule

Never select whole-model adoption merely because weights are available.

Whole-model retention is allowed only if minimization proves that the complete retained dependency is the smallest sufficient winning route under the optimization objective.

## Reuse and deletion rule

Before harvesting:
- remove knowledge that belongs in JIT retrieval;
- remove capabilities already owned by Brain;
- remove functions better served by deterministic/software mechanisms;
- remove redundant donor scope from the target contract.

After harvesting:
- recursively minimize the capsule;
- factor duplicated shared learned substrate across capsules when verified;
- delete donor dependencies that are not causally necessary.

## Opaque-capability rule

If an external model/service is superior but the target capability remains locked inside inaccessible hidden behavior, classify:
EXTERNAL_CAPABILITY_SPECIMEN_FOUND__INTERNALIZATION_NOT_YET_ACHIEVED.

Do not promote.

## Completion

A target is complete only at VERIFIED_OWNED_EQUAL_OR_BETTER after the exact Brain-configured route itself passes, including donor-deletion proof when harvesting was used.
