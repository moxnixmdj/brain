## Route race

Race mechanisms, not providers.

Candidate classes:
1. already-owned Brain capability configuration;
2. deletion/bypass of unnecessary capability demand;
3. deterministic or software substitute;
4. open skills/procedure bundles;
5. agent scaffolds and algorithmic workflows;
6. prompt/policy/tool configurations;
7. published/exposed reusable methods;
8. white-box donor harvesting;
9. compositions/configurations of mechanisms;
10. invention or architecture redesign when V9 admissibility is satisfied;
11. opaque external models/services only as specimens.

The winning route minimizes verified wall-clock to owned equal-or-better capability, subject to correctness, ownership, spend, and reliability constraints.

## White-box donor race

For a surviving learned residual, choose donors by extraction value, not prestige.

Prefer exact target strength, legal/technical inspectability, causal contrasts, modularity, transferable residual structure, and low extraction/verification cost.

## Bytes race

Before teaching or retaining large learned machinery:
- test zero-byte composition;
- test elicitation/control artifacts;
- test sparse/low-rank/small modules;
- simultaneously shrink any donor from the top;
- localize and acquire only the first irreducible missing transformation;
- rerun before adding the next residual.

After multiple capabilities exist, factor shared substrate and solve the minimum unique-byte dependency-closed cover over the verified mechanism library.

## Dominance rule

A specimen can dominate discovery but cannot win ownership.

A donor can dominate extraction but cannot remain the hidden promoted target-capability provider.

An invented mechanism can win only after the exact same acceptance gates.

## No-whole-model-default rule

Never select whole-model adoption merely because weights are available.

Whole-model retention is allowed only if resource-bounded minimization shows that the complete retained dependency is the winning sufficient route.

## Reuse, deletion, and dependency obsolescence

Before harvesting or invention:
- remove JIT knowledge;
- remove capabilities already owned;
- remove deterministic/software-replaceable functions;
- remove redundant target scope.

After sufficiency:
- recursively minimize;
- delete incidental package dependencies;
- factor duplicated shared substrate;
- rerun verification after material deletions.

## Optimality claim rule

Exact minimum claims are allowed only over explicitly enumerated, verified candidate classes or mechanism libraries.

Do not upgrade a finite-library optimum into a universal minimum claim.

## Completion

A target is complete only at VERIFIED_OWNED_EQUAL_OR_BETTER after the exact Brain route passes, including donor deletion when harvesting was used.
