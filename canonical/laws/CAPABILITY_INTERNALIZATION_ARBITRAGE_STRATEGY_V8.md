## Objective

Minimize wall-clock to a sound Brain-owned terminal capability, subject to correctness, >= Opus 5.5, donor independence, hard $0 incremental spend, and fail-closed verification.

## Route race

For each load-bearing ABC residual, race:
1. theorem/deletion/bypass;
2. existing Brain-owned route;
3. deterministic/tool/software route;
4. JIT information plus owned generic mechanism;
5. composition or configuration;
6. exposed reusable mechanism;
7. white-box harvesting;
8. invention or architecture redesign;
9. external opaque systems only as specimens/evaluators.

Route class identity has no intrinsic priority. Select by expected verified terminal information/capability gain per wall-clock under hard correctness constraints.

## Selection score

Use a qualitative or quantitative ranking equivalent to:

expected terminal residual reduction
× probability of sound success
÷
(wall-clock + verification burden + external dependency risk + contamination risk + durable complexity)

Correctness, ownership, $0 spend, and target semantics are hard gates, not soft weights.

## Invention/redesign

Invention is favored when it deletes more dependency or proof burden than acquisition and can be verified faster.

Examples include:
- a deterministic exact solver replacing learned behavior;
- a new general query/experiment selector replacing class-specific stored separators;
- a new architecture that turns multiple role-specific gaps into one shared verified mechanism;
- a direct absolute-ceiling algorithm that bypasses target-comparator access.

Invention is disfavored when the target semantics are not yet known well enough to judge success.

## Donor race

Use white-box donors only when extraction is faster than reuse/invention and the donor can be removed from the terminal runtime.

Rank donors by causal extraction value, modularity, inspectability, license, transferability, and verification cost, not prestige.

## Target-information boundary

Do not confuse inability to prove Opus occupancy of an escape cell with a missing Brain mechanism.

If the residual is epistemic, acquire or derive only the minimum target information needed to exclude the undominated region.

If the residual is a semantically complete, causally localized Brain capability deficit, race reuse, extraction, and invention immediately.

## Completion

A route wins only at VERIFIED_OWNED_EQUAL_OR_BETTER on exact Brain-owned execution. Specimen performance, donor performance, or architectural elegance is not completion.
