## Route race

Race existing **capability configurations**, not merely providers.

Candidate classes:
1. already-owned Brain capability configuration;
2. open skills/procedure bundles;
3. agent scaffolds;
4. software/algorithmic workflows;
5. prompt/policy/tool configurations;
6. published/exposed reusable methods;
7. compositions of existing mechanisms;
8. opaque external models/services only as capability specimens.

## Dominance rule

A specimen can dominate discovery but cannot win ownership.

The winning ownership route is the fastest route whose operative mechanism becomes a Brain-owned configuration and whose Brain execution meets/exceeds Opus 5.5.

## Opaque-capability rule

If an external model/service is superior but the target capability remains locked inside its hidden behavior, classify:
EXTERNAL_CAPABILITY_SPECIMEN_FOUND__INTERNALIZATION_NOT_YET_ACHIEVED.

Do not promote.

## Reuse rule

Prefer existing exposed skills/configurations over reconstructing behavior from micro-primitives.

## Completion

A target is complete only at VERIFIED_OWNED_EQUAL_OR_BETTER after the Brain-configured route itself passes.
