# GENERAL CAPABILITY FIRST DEVELOPMENT LAW V1

Status: CANONICAL / MANDATORY / FAIL-CLOSED

## Parent objective

The sole development objective is:

`BUILD_THE_MOST_CAPABLE_GENERAL_RELIABLE_AND_RESOURCE_EFFICIENT_PROBLEM_SOLVING_SYSTEM_POSSIBLE`

No benchmark, model family, acceptance row, score threshold, leaderboard rank, frozen protocol, or named frontier system may replace this parent objective.

## Development order

Every consequential development action must follow this order:

1. Observe a real or causally valid failure, limitation, or opportunity that bears on general problem-solving capability.
2. Search the Brain for an already verified mechanism that can be reused, adapted, composed, configured, or promoted.
3. Prefer the smallest general repair with the greatest reusable capability gain per unit wall-clock, compute, memory, cost, complexity, and evidence risk.
4. Verify on the exact claimed scope plus neighboring, adversarial, transfer, or real-task cases where applicable.
5. Integrate verified capability into the live Brain.
6. Generalize, compress, and reuse it.
7. Use benchmarks and frontier-system comparisons only as audits, falsifiers, calibration surfaces, or causal probes.

## Benchmark anti-Goodhart rule

A benchmark may authorize development work only when at least one is true:

- it exposes a causally localized general capability defect;
- it provides independent audit evidence for a general capability claim;
- it is the cheapest nondominated probe for a currently unresolved general capability question.

A red benchmark row, score gap, acceptance-family gap, or rank is not by itself development authority.

Score improvement, threshold satisfaction, certificate closure, or family closure receives zero general-development credit unless it proves or verifies a reusable capability improvement.

Benchmark-specific patches that do not survive transfer/generalization are audit artifacts only and may not be promoted as Brain capability.

## Frontier-system role

Opus 5.5 and every other frontier system are checkpoints and comparison specimens, not architectural optimization targets. Their behaviors may reveal useful capabilities to internalize or falsify Brain claims, but matching a named system is subordinate to building the strongest generally reusable system.

## Reuse before invention

The route order is:

DELETE -> BYPASS -> REUSE -> ADAPT -> SUBSTITUTE -> COMPOSE -> CONFIGURE -> INTEGRATE -> SYNTHESIZE_NEW_PRIMITIVE.

A new primitive is admissible only after the relevant existing mechanisms and transformations have been specifically falsified or proved insufficient for the claimed scope.

## Progress credit

Development progress requires at least one of:

- verified reusable capability gain;
- verified reliability, generalization, or resource-efficiency gain without capability regression;
- verified integration that makes an already-owned capability available through the live Brain;
- independent falsification that removes a false general claim and causally narrows the frontier.

Activity, benchmark score movement alone, proof bureaucracy, and repeated certification of unchanged behavior are not progress.

## Scheduler objective

Choose the nondominated action maximizing expected:

`GENERAL_CAPABILITY_GAIN * REUSE_BREADTH * RELIABILITY_GAIN * CAUSAL_CONFIDENCE`

per:

`WALL_CLOCK * COMPUTE * MEMORY * COST * COMPLEXITY * EVIDENCE_RISK`.

Correctness and fail-closed truth remain hard constraints, not tradeable score components.

## Development loop

UNDERSTAND -> PLAN -> COMPOSE -> ACT -> VERIFY -> LEARN -> GENERALIZE -> COMPRESS -> REUSE.

Benchmark audits may enter the loop as observations. They do not become the loop's goal.
