# Global Terminal Proof-Tree Collapse Law V1

Status: CANONICAL_FAIL_CLOSED_INFORMATION_MINIMIZATION_LAW

## Objective

Close the terminal objective with the fewest genuinely new bits of reality, not by marching family-by-family, benchmark-by-benchmark, or rank-by-rank.

The unit of work is a terminal proof obligation. Capability-family names and benchmark ranks are evaluation/accounting surfaces, not scheduling authority.

## Global scope rule

Before selecting, exposing, executing, or verifying any untouched qualification task, the controller MUST operate over the complete set of currently open terminal proof obligations across all open capability families.

A family-local or benchmark-local next action is admissible only if it belongs to the current GLOBAL minimum reality cut or is required to construct that cut from already-existing evidence without spending untouched information.

## Untouched information exposure is reality acquisition

The following are fresh reality/information acquisition and therefore require admission before they occur:

- selecting an untouched task as the next qualification spend when selection itself consumes scarce clean evidence;
- reading/fetching an untouched task's official instruction;
- reading task-specific hints, tests, solutions, hidden-verifier material, or non-allowlisted task-specific external evidence;
- executing a task;
- invoking a terminal verifier;
- collecting a new environment or runtime observation that is not already implied by admissible evidence.

Stage-A cleanliness does NOT authorize Stage-B instruction exposure by itself. Cleanliness proves the sample is usable; global minimum-reality admission proves it is necessary.

## Required order

1. Build/refresh the complete terminal obligation graph from the terminal manifest and behavioral leaf registry.
2. Saturate every open obligation with admissible existing Brain and external evidence.
3. Apply deductive closure globally.
4. Compress duplicate/shared residual mechanisms across family boundaries.
5. Build counterfactual worlds only for the remaining terminal-relevant unknowns.
6. Delete impossible and dominated mechanisms, architectures, tests, and task exposures.
7. Compute the exact global minimum reality cut.
8. Admit only observations in that cut.
9. Execute independent cut members in parallel.
10. Compile every result into reusable evidence and recompute the entire cut.

## Anti-serialization rule

Do not finish one family merely because it is currently active if another shared mechanism or observation can close more terminal obligations per unit wall-clock or new information.

Do not allocate fixed test counts per family.

Do not advance benchmark rank N to N+1 merely because N finished.

## Existing evidence priority

If a terminal obligation can be closed from existing receipts, source code, formal deduction, historical failures, public benchmark artifacts, papers, datasets, official documentation, independent reproductions, or a proven stronger/equivalent scope, delete the new experiment.

## Global cut admission receipt

Any untouched task selection/exposure must reference a current receipt containing:

- complete open-obligation inventory or a cryptographically/deterministically bound graph snapshot;
- existing-evidence saturation result;
- deductive closure result;
- shared-residual compression result;
- candidate observations and their covered distinctions;
- dominance deletions;
- exact minimum-reality-cut result;
- explicit membership of the proposed observation;
- terminal decision value;
- contamination and authority constraints.

Unknown or missing fields fail closed.

## Rank-23 correction

The independent rank-23 Stage-A receipt proves contamination cleanliness and generic pre-exposure admissibility only. It contains no global minimum-reality-cut admission. Because the official instruction remains unread, Stage-B exposure is revoked without spending the sample.

Rank-23 may be re-admitted only if the global cut proves that exposing its instruction is an irreducible observation.

## Exactness scope hardening

An exact global minimum is not a meaningful claim over an open-ended, undiscovered action universe.

Therefore every claim of an exact global minimum reality cut MUST be explicitly relative to a frozen finite universe and frozen cost model. Before exact-cut computation, the controller must freeze and hash:

- the complete current open terminal-obligation graph;
- the evidence-saturation receipt;
- the finite admissible candidate proof-action universe;
- the discovery frontier that produced that universe;
- the cost model used to compare new reality and wall-clock;
- each candidate action's complete internal dependency bundle.

The canonical compiler is `canonical/runtime/global_proof_cut_compiler.py`, verified by `canonical/verification/GLOBAL_PROOF_CUT_COMPILER_FROZEN_UNIVERSE_VERIFICATION_20261002_V1.json`.

The exactness label is:

`EXACT_RELATIVE_TO_FROZEN_DECLARED_CANDIDATE_UNIVERSE_AND_COST_MODEL`

and never means that no undiscovered strategy could exist.

Any newly discovered candidate action, newly discovered terminal obligation, changed cost model, or invalidated evidence receipt immediately invalidates the freeze and requires global recomputation before new untouched information is spent.

Independent admitted cut members are parallel. Wall-clock optimization therefore uses the maximum declared critical-path wall-clock of selected independent bundles, not the arithmetic sum of their wall clocks.


## Benchmark-output information equivalence

For every benchmark-linked obligation, the benchmark procedure is subordinate to the terminal decision information it is meant to provide.

Before native benchmark execution, classify the terminal predicate as either:

- `CAPABILITY_RELATION`: determine a capability ordering or measurement. Prefer an exact sufficient statistic or a proof-carrying stronger certificate when it determines the same decision with less new information.
- `EXACT_BENCHMARK_STATISTIC`: determine the benchmark's exact realized score/statistic. A capability proof cannot substitute unless a formal exact-statistic bridge binds the frozen scorer state.

The mandatory fail-closed rule is defined in:

`canonical/laws/BENCHMARK_OUTPUT_INFORMATION_EQUIVALENCE_LAW_V1.md`

and enforced structurally by:

`canonical/runtime/benchmark_output_information_equivalence_gate_v1.py`

Native benchmark execution has no scheduling privilege. Proxy metrics, private-answer reconstruction, target-output leakage, and silent switching between capability and exact-score predicates are forbidden.
