## Terminal objective

Permanently internalize, configure, verify, and own the useful real-world capabilities **demonstrated by GPT-6 Astra**, at equal-or-better real outcome strength, with knowledge external/JIT only, donor-independent owned runtime capability, hard $0 incremental spend, and fail-closed verification.

The capability is the target. Model names, benchmark names, predicates, scores, and receipts are evidence or comparison instruments only. They are never the mission.

## Critical separation theorem

The target universe and the comparison evidence MUST be represented as different objects.

- **Target universe U_Astra**: useful behaviors Astra is authoritatively demonstrated to perform.
- **Strength relation R_b**: the outcome relation used to decide whether Brain is equal-or-better for target behavior b.
- **Evidence E_b**: benchmarks, demonstrations, primary-source claims, formal proofs, exhaustive tests, or matched evaluations used to establish R_b.
- **Owned witness P_b**: content-addressed donor-independent Brain route implementing b.

A benchmark row may support E_b. It cannot create, delete, broaden, or narrow b by itself.

## Why this corrects the current terminal-finality trap

"All useful capabilities of a model" is open-world unless the capability universe is independently sealed. A finite benchmark/predicate registry cannot prove that hidden or unenumerated useful behaviors do not exist.

"Astra-demonstrated capabilities in a frozen authoritative demonstration corpus" is a finite evidentiary target universe. Once the corpus and extraction rule are frozen, U_Astra is mechanically enumerable. New authoritative Astra demonstrations reopen the universe; they do not retroactively make an old finite proof universal.

Therefore the terminal certifier has two distinct duties:

1. prove target-universe completeness relative to the frozen authoritative Astra demonstration corpus;
2. prove an owned equal-or-better witness for every target behavior in that universe.

This removes the invalid inference from "all currently registered predicates passed" to "all useful capabilities are owned."
