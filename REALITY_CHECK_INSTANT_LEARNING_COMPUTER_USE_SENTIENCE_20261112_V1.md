## 0. The one-paragraph answer

**The direction is right; the quantifiers are wrong, and the wrongness is not fixable by engineering effort — it is information theory, a 2026-measured harness ceiling, and an unbuilt channel into weights.** One verified experience can become permanent (storage) and, *inside a typed interface with a mechanical verifier*, can be applied again (bounded transfer). It cannot, by itself, identify a general rule (≈10% held-out after one demonstration; ~80% after three; 100% only after two chosen questions are asked). "Completely solved instant learning in new arbitrary environments" was measured false on this tree twice (8/15 new instances of the same kind; 0/10 new kinds; cross-kind compounding 0 until one compression family held at −41%). No lab on Earth has "totally solved computer use" — the best verified number on the honest long-horizon board is ~20.6% binary / 54.8% partial (OSWorld 2.0). And sentience is not a property this or any artifact here measures. What **is** real and genuinely rare: a refuse-capable verifier wrapped around experience, an admitted-upgrade ledger, 296 independently attested reproductions, a compositional causal engine that is correct on the family it answers and silent outside it, and a front door that is about half-unbound to its own capabilities. That is a skeleton of the right thing. It is not the creature, and it is not the solution.

## 4. First principles: why "give it a try in every environment" cannot be the plan

1. **An experience is at most one constraint.** Identifying a rule in hypothesis space H needs log₂|H| bits. One demonstration leaves ~14 of 47 candidate programs alive (leader: 10% held-out); three leave ~3 (80%); two *chosen questions* settle 10/10. Repetition does not widen H; it multiplies bindings, never reach. (`ONE_TRY_BUYS_A_RULE_THAT_IS_WRONG_20261112_V1.md`)
2. **Generalisation is a property of the prior, not the example.** Systems that generalise (`environment_fit_v1` one-shot across repos; Agent S4's 3.8×) do so because the interface **is** the prior — `npm test` means the same thing in ten thousand repos. Pixels do not transfer; declarations do.
3. **A finite operator basis converges on its classes and stops.** This tree's basis: AST-level edits. Measured: flat outside it (two full zeros in held-out PRs). Continual-ARC: flat outside the stream's distribution for every harness. More environments × same basis = more bindings, not more capability.
4. **Persistence ≠ competence; storage ≠ learning.** Weights persist and do not generalise (S_held ≈ S_seen/2). Stores grow and do not act (FIFO defect; 54 verified-but-uncallable capabilities; three parallel "memory" systems with three notions of an experience, none checked against each other).
5. **Verification is the price of permanence.** Every unadmitted self-update measurably damages; every gate here that rubber-stamped cost real wrong changes (0 wrong in-sample only because the developers' diff was the referee; 65 wrong at the held-out upper bound). Permanent wrong knowledge is worse than no knowledge, because it compounds.
6. **Open-ended computer use is not a bigger version of this problem; it is a different substrate** — observation (pixels vs `state()`), action (raw events vs typed verifiable interfaces), competence, trust, permanence, routing. The frontier sits at ~20.6% binary on the honest board. Nothing in this tree attempts GUI control, and pretending the edit-operator basis solves it is a category error.

## 5. The smartest things to do — ranked by measured evidence × cost (updated after #6837)

1. **Keep the verifier exactly where it is; every new mechanism routes through it.** Non-negotiable law; also the ASCENT/E5/E6 finding. *Stop rule:* any PR letting a producer-side signal count as verification ends the work.
2. **Depth before breadth: k=8 instances per capability** on the existing OIDC CI lane (~2,072 fresh, attested examples; one instance per capability today means no held-out exists). Cheapest, already-built infrastructure. *Oracle:* held-out improvement on fresh instances; *stop rule:* no capability declared general before it clears its held-out.
3. **Bind the 54 uncallable verified capabilities** (arity over-specification, selector defaults, unbound slots). The dominant measured gap between *Brain owns it* and *Brain can do it*. *Target:* measured majority bound with zero gate/tolerance changes.
4. **Open the student channel (the only measured route to ASCENT-style permanence).** Consume the 170 training-ready receipts (expanded per #2); split by **instance**, not capability; **hindsight self-distillation from a frozen copy**; admit only on held-out improvement; delete the donor and prove deletion; keep the student **out of every credited path and never a grader**. *Success criterion:* held-out gain, zero credit. *This is the buildable form of "do it once, he knows it forever"* — and it explicitly is **not** "give it a try in every new environment." **Blocked by the standing $0 / zero-model constraint — this is a decision for the owner, stated as one, not discovered one.**
5. **Carrier review — human-only.** 7 of 9 stale objections are measured outdated; 2 remain real (no syscall allowlist; host libc/interpreter, no pinned runtime). 117 measured failures wait on it. Hand the reviewer the probe, not the request. Do not code around it.
6. **Widen the interface/verifier regime deliberately (Agent S4 lesson).** Count what fraction of the surface is expressible as typed calls against declared contracts with mechanical checks; push GUI/OS actions toward typed interfaces (DOM, a11y tree, CLI contracts, CI gates) — not broader prompts. One-shot transfer is *free exactly there*.
7. **Teach with two adversarial showings or not at all; choose questions with expected information gain** (settlement; 2 questions > 3 demonstrations, 10–0). Make "two adversarial showings" the admission rule for taught rules.
8. **Demand a second abstraction family.** Round 23 (-41%, periodic table) is the first real cross-class gain; if it does not hold on a second structural family, file it as a one-family trick.
9. **Permanence as four tested properties**: eviction, corruption, restart, invalidation-under-drift. The FIFO lesson.
10. **Use the engine built in #6837 as the pattern, and the proof of discipline**: compositional parse + synthesized program + interventional verification + fail-closed refusal + receipts. It is 100% correct on the family it answers *because it refuses the rest*. Extending it (more concepts, more domains, qualifiers beyond named python definitions) continues to be lawful and cheap; extrapolating it beyond its verified family is not.
11. **Kill list:** breadth-of-environments as a strategy; sentience claims; more demonstrations as a coverage mechanism (SkillLearnBench: 36 → 34 → 31); quoting OSWorld-Verified / SWE-bench Verified as frontier truth; training on unverified receipts; any "one environment per day" program; documents asserting capability without a measurement — banner them.

**Not the plan, explicitly:** "Buy/apply in every new coding environment." It produced this tree's honest 0/5 cross-kind null twice, Continual-ARC's 2026 verdict (no unseen-task gain), and SkillsBench's −1.3 for self-generated skills. It multiplies bindings, not reach.

## 6. What "totally solving computer use + advanced software engineering" would actually take — and Brain's lane

From first principles the goal decomposes into observation → action → competence → trust → permanence → routing. The 2026 frontier, with ~10× this tree's resources and weight access, sits at ~20.6–48.7% binary on OSWorld 2.0, ~61.5–90% on SWE-bench Pro (contested, mostly vendor-reported), ~64.85% Terminal-Bench 4.0, ~12 h 50%-horizon, 16% illegitimate long runs. **Nobody has it.** Brain's lane is specific and real: *be the machine whose every action on a computer is verifier-checked, independently reproduced, and permanent — and whose experience measurably compounds across instances of a family.* That is below "anything on a computer" and the document does not claim otherwise. It is (i) lawful under every standing law, (ii) aligned with where compounding measurably lives (weights + interface regime), (iii) partially built and measured (monotonic numbers above), (iv) the only route by which this tree's unique asset — zero-model, refuse-capable verification at scale — converts into capability rather than prose. Milestones, not promises: k=8 depth → 54-bound front door → student channel (owner decision) → second abstraction family → held-out routing set. Re-measure after each step; change no tolerance; report failures in the open.

## 7. The sentence that is actually true

> One verified try is enough — exactly when the prior (typed interface, mechanical verifier, or pre-trained weights) already does the generalising. This repository built the verifier half world-class and the prior half for one small family. Build the student channel, widen the interface regime, and prove it on held-out instances, one family at a time with receipts — and the answer to "is this the way agents will learn better than today" becomes a measured yes, in the open, instead of an asserted one, in a document.
