**2026-11-12, second asking.** Branch `arena/bab32a2a-brain`. Authority: **NONE**.
`truth.achieved` stays `false`. $0 incremental spend. Zero model dependency in every
result-bearing path. This is an addendum, not a duplicate: the full scorecard, the 2026
frontier evidence and the ranked plan live in `WHAT_2026_SAYS_ABOUT_THIS_20261112_V1.md`
(merged in #6734, on `main`). This note does two things only: (a) updates it with what the
tree measured **today, hours later**, and (b) answers the question's literal sharpest form,
which is new since that document landed.

> The owner asks again: *Why can't we just give our model the most advanced coding actions
> and let him experience them once and then he will learn them? Can't we do this now?
> After we do this we have the most advanced coding agent on the planet.*

**Short answer: we can get most of the way there, and the wall that remains is not where
you think it is. The actions exist, the door now reaches most of them, and the machine
refuses to run them *ungoverned* — that refusal is the design working, and this evening it
was given its constructive answer. But "do this now" is still a sentence about a gate, not
about a new capability: until the confinement seam is admitted through the door, the
advanced-coding actions are registered, reachable and inert by law; and even with the seam
wired, one try buys one literal edit, not a general rule, because the corpus of real inputs
is empty by construction. What you are asking for is not one mechanism away. It is a chain
of five, each measured, each priced below — and the first link is small, additive and
already built this evening.**

## 2. The chain between "give it a try" and "it learns permanently"

Each link, with its measured state and its price. This is the whole answer to "why can't we
do this now" — skipping a link does not skip its cost.

**Link 1 — The actions exist.** Yes. 145 bound capabilities, including the exact
advanced-coding operators the owner names (repair loops, localization, cross-file edits,
synthesize-from-transform). Not one of them is "too advanced to register."

**Link 2 — The door reaches them.** Mostly yes, as of this evening. The first binding
surface refused 54 act-level calls; the second surface (`live_brain_route_admission_v1.solve`
with `declared_input_derivation_v1`) resolved most of those — 26 of the former refusals now
flow past compile at the door, and under realistic, material-aware sentences 3 of 38
advanced-coding goals compiled to themselves and executed with verification. The earlier
"54 uncallable" number in the morning's document is now historical, not current.

**Link 3 — The machine refuses to run them *ungoverned*.** This is the link being mistaken
for a defect, and it is the load-bearing one. 19 of 38 realistic advanced-coding probes
refuse with exactly `FAIL_CLOSED__COMPUTER_EXECUTION_REQUIRES_ADMITTED_CONFINEMENT`. The
wall is the absence of an *admitted* confinement with effects verified — not a bug, not a
palette gap, not a model. This evening's round **built that substrate**:
`confinement_v1.py`, capped worktree, restricted environment, budget, sha256-verified
declared effects, host untouched, receipts content-addressed, 9/9 measured. **It is not
wired.** The boundary's refusal sites are named in its docstring; the fix is deliberately
additive: a confinement receipt satisfies the boundary's release condition; no receipt →
the identical refusal as today. Until that diff lands, "can't we do this now" has one
honest word: **the machine keeps its word.**

**Link 4 — One try ≠ learned, even then.** With Link 3 done: one showing of a code edit
reaches **1 of 4** unseen sites (the model-free learner refuses the rest, honestly); two
adversarially different showings reach **4 of 4**; a structural change (add a statement) is
refused outright. A single *successful run* of an action teaches one literal instance — the
tree's own measurements: the permanent store holds **1 episode, 3 macros, 7 lessons
(3 procedures), 0 settled experience resolutions**, and **532 receipts carry one recorded
argument** (a corpus entry is the inputs of a real task). There is no cheap seeding: fixtures
fail all 56 file-input capabilities (0/56), because an environment is a place to run, not an
instance to learn from. **The price of a corpus entry is real work with a verifier attached**
— the same price the 2026 record pays (Continual-ARC streams, ASCENT's one-pass-per-task).
Two thousand instances means two thousand real, attested executions.

**Link 5 — Even learned lessons must compose and persist, measured, not assumed.**
Two individually-correct rules applied in sequence can undo each other; the old
`recall_and_apply_verified` verifies each step locally and reports PASS for a global revert
(`SKILLS_DONT_COMPOSE_20261112_V1.md`; 15 hardcoded `whole_task_correctness_proved` fields
are read **zero** times). And permanence is a decay rate: a lesson is permanent only if it
still fits the tree when replayed next month — replay-on-schedule, with a published fit rate,
is the only defensible meaning of "permanent." The store census today is the cautionary
number: almost nothing is retained, not because memory fails but because **almost nothing
reaches the door to be done**.

**Link 6 — The field's verdict, for the record.** Static libraries of demonstrated actions:
inert (SWE-Skills-Bench: 92/98 skills no significant effect; harmful skills anchor the agent
on their own template). Self-distilled skill libraries: unreliable after authoring cost
(EvoClawBench). Task-specific synthesis from a few retrieved traces: real, large (SkillTTA:
+10.8 / +13.4). Harness editing under a frozen evaluator: the largest published lever (SICA
17→53; Darwin Gödel Machine 20→50 on SWE-bench) — and in this repo that lever sits behind
the owner's protected-path gate by design.

**Link 7 — "Our model."** There isn't one, and the laws keep it so until it is earned.
No Brain-credited result may depend on another model's cognition (hosting doesn't launder;
unknown provenance fails closed). `0 of 145` registered capabilities update weights. The
$0 spend rule forbids buying the GPU an ASCENT-style student loop needs. The only lawful
weights path is the one already named: acquisition-time donor, hindsight self-distillation
on the tree's verified receipts, held-out admission, donor deletion with proof, student
never credited and never a grader — and only when the owner funds or frees the compute.

So, to the literal question — *why can't we just give the model the most advanced coding
actions and experience them once?* — **because the sequence "try → keep → apply everywhere"
is not one act; it is Links 3 through 6 in order, and Links 3 and 4 have not happened yet,
while Link 7 cannot happen at all under the current spend and law. The revolution you are
asking for is real, and its price is named.**

## 5. Kill list (now with external measurement, not just argument)

1. **Static action/skill catalogues as the bet** — SWE-Skills-Bench: 92/98 inert, ±17 tails,
   harmful skills anchor agents. (This tree's store is already the counter-example.)
2. **Self-distilled skill libraries as a general bet** — EvoClawBench: no reliable gain after
   authoring cost; runtime-dependent collapses.
3. **Experience-without-a-corpus as if it were evidence** — 1 episode, 3 macros, 7 lessons,
   0 resolutions; 532 receipts / 1 argument; 296 attestations / 0 arguments. No instance, no learning.
4. **Steps verified locally treated as sequences verified globally** — two individually-correct
   rules cancel each other; local checks pass; global revert invisible. Use `verify_sequence`.
5. **"Rewire the door/boundary to make the test green"** — the refusal *is* the safety
   property; open it only by admitting a confinement receipt, never by deleting the refusal.
6. **"It learned it, permanently"** — until it is replayed against the future tree and counted;
   permanence is a fit-rate, not a feeling.
7. **Sentience, and any vocabulary that implies it** — nothing measures it; the laws and this
   repo's non-claims exist so that fact stays visible.
8. **Buying breadth** (more environments, more fixtures) — the corpse is measured twice
   (0/5, 1525=1525) and now a third time by the field.
