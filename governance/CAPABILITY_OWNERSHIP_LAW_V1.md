# Capability Ownership Law V1

A benchmark result may be credited to Brain only when the task-level cognition that produces the answer is Brain-owned at evaluation time.

External general-purpose models may be used during research or capability acquisition, but they may not remain load-bearing for reasoning, planning, judgment, tool choice, task interpretation, or answer generation in the credited runtime.

Rehosting, quantizing, wrapping, or routing through an external model does not make that model's cognition a Brain capability.

Capability transfer is admissible only after the external teacher is removed and the derived Brain-owned artifact independently passes fresh qualification, causal ablation/rescue, dependency closure, and leakage checks.

Non-cognitive algorithms and narrow sensors are allowed. For example, OCR may transcribe visible text, but it may not perform task-level reasoning or answer generation.

For Chartography specifically, any result whose runtime task reasoning comes from another general-purpose model is noncreditable as Brain capability.

If the donor-free Brain does not meet the benchmark target, the correct status is UNSOLVED.
