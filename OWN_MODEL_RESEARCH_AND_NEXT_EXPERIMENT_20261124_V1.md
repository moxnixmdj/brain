# Own-model research and next experiment — 2026-11-24

Status: **open; producer-run research and regression tests only.** No independent verification, new frontier score, demonstrated arbitrary-computer competence, or terminal credit. Branch `arena/d582a17b-brain`. This is a research and engineering record, not a qualification receipt.

## Bound requirements and interpretation

The aim is an own system that can pursue unfamiliar software, terminal, browser and desktop goals, using no other model for runtime cognition, retaining and generalizing experience, and obtaining missing knowledge as needed. It must be evaluated on unseen tasks without hand-solving, leaked answers, or external-model outputs. Maintain one branch and merge only after the user's completion conditions and repository checks hold. Respect the existing fail-closed Harbor boundary: explicit opt-in is a request, not evidence of confinement.

No warehouse of world facts in model parameters is compatible with durable learning if *facts, experiences and candidate skills* live in a separate inspectable, editable store, not in fixed weights. Mechanisms and inductive biases still contain design information; zero stored parameters does not mean zero prior assumptions. The current mechanisms discussed below are code and external JSON, not a demonstrated from-scratch trained neural model. Network access to information is not a runtime model call, but retrieved material is untrusted and does not authorize actions. No blanket proof that all live runtime paths satisfy these rules exists.

## What the live tree actually does

- `canonical/swe_kernel/perfect_algorithm_v1.py` already composes resettable-environment scan, a novelty/closeness ranking, a shortest path over **observed** transitions, execution, checking, and a store. `canonical/runtime/environment_learning_v1.py` supplies the scan. The interface requires caller-provided actions, state, reset and a goal predicate; those do not automatically exist for an arbitrary GUI or natural-language goal. Planning is shortest only within the learned graph, not globally fastest.
- Despite its name, `right_question` is a heuristic, not a measured or optimized expected-information-gain calculation. It ranks novelty at the root, but probing occurs at the environment's current state after the scan, which may not be the root. Its output is logged, not subsequently used to drive further questioning. `canonical/runtime/settlement_v1.py` does implement expected information gain for explicitly supplied hypotheses and probes; applying it to arbitrary computer use remains unproven.
- The local-guide/JIT retrieval in this loop is attempted **after** the planner fails. The loop records a summary and returns `OPEN__NO_PLAN_AFTER_SCAN`; it does not convert the retrieved text into a plan and retry. `jit_knowledge_v1.resolve` labels `complete` when counts of sources and source kinds meet thresholds. Those counts do **not** establish relevance, factual completeness, independence or agreement: e.g. a GitHub repository and its README can be counted under different kinds. Local guide keyword overlap likewise cannot establish correctness. Do not use these labels as proof or authorization. Other live routes inspect retrieved executable names, so a whole-system safety audit remains necessary.
- The unified model's `solve` is a deterministic capability router, not evidence of a general program/action generator. Earlier boundary tests show it is invoked on the host, not inside Harbor. No verified container-local solver or fresh-verifier integration is available here. The refusal remains in place.
- Existing memory admits rules only under its particular tests; an admitted rule can still be wrong elsewhere, and replay is not a general-purpose safety guarantee. The 2026-11-22 replay receipt used identical-shape instances and explicitly says replay did not learn anything new. A different-layout grid receipt discarded 43 prior transitions, rechecked 3 root transitions and did not persist a plan rule. Both are producer runs.

## Learning verdict

**Real but bounded:** storing an episode or a verified procedure and reusing it in a similar environment can be durable and useful. Earlier project reports measured 8/15 one-trial within-kind transfers under their specific setup, not arbitrary transfer. An observable-condition study reported correct decisions on its constructed latent-action states but required 13 source learning executions and 112 target evaluation oracles; that is not one-shot autonomous mastery.

**False as a universal guarantee:** one finite demonstration cannot determine an arbitrary unseen rule. Two environments can produce the same observations and require opposite actions on the next state. Choosing between them requires assumptions, further information, or a risk of error. The repository's constructed identifiability probe had 0/10 exact identifications from one example and 10/10 after an average of two selected questions within its restricted hypothesis space. This is an illustration, not a universal performance guarantee.

**Unproven at frontier:** in the producer-only HumanEvalFix experience stream, own memory repaired 64/100 and was right on 45 as far as checked, versus 62/100 and 42 for a deliberately backwards-memory control. The project adjudicator reported `NO_COMPOUNDING`: 1.0819x mean cost improvement against its 1.5x criterion, with 31/98 paired wins. Published fixes helped repeated-instance recall but did not establish learning to solve the next arbitrary bug. The score was not independently replicated and is not a frontier benchmark.

An advanced single experience may encode many transitions, but it can still lack contrasting failures and cannot supply perception, action grounding, causal models, unseen domain concepts, or a sound verifier that the system cannot already represent. Whether a particular single experience transfers is an empirical question, not implied by its difficulty.

## External research, used as ideas rather than runtime cognition

- [SkillLearnBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.20087v1) evaluates 20 tasks across 15 subdomains with deterministic verification. In its **LLM-based** experiments, generated skills helped on average but no method led everywhere; open-ended tasks could worsen and external feedback mattered. This is not evidence that this no-model system achieves those results.
- [Origin-bound memory authority](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.24322) analyzes memory poisoning and laundering. Its formal claims hold under the paper's model; they do not certify this repository. Origin labels, least privilege and explicit action authorization are required independently of retrieved text.
- [ARC-AGI-3 technical report](https://arcprize.org/media/ARC_AGI_3_Technical_Report.pdf) separates public demonstration from private evaluation. Existing local `canonical/verification/ARC_AGI_3_PUBLIC_EXPLORATION_20261111_V1.json` is a producer-only earlier public-run digest, with a documented Python-version deviation. At 20x baseline budget, existing `as_is` averaged 0.67 levels out of 183 over 3 runs; random averaged 3.33. These are public exploratory scores, not a held-out private result or GUI/coding benchmark. `arc_agi` and `arcengine` are not installed now.
- [OSWorld-V2](https://github.com/xlang-ai/OSWorld-V2) recommends its versioned OSWorld 2.1 release for long-horizon computer use; [FrontierSWE v2](https://github.com/Proximal-Labs/frontier-swe-v2/tree/da83f84f8fbcec3cbf0f2b17c98ebc811355c2df) has 34 long-horizon software/scientific tasks, sometimes GPU-dependent. Neither was run in this work. Different benchmarks test different capabilities; no unique hardest benchmark is asserted.

## One minimal direction and experiment

**Goal-directed exploration, with bounded exploration to test assumptions**, not an exhaustive scan: enumerate available, authorized affordances; form explicit competing task-relevant hypotheses; rank safe probes by expected decision value divided by cost when a likelihood model exists, and otherwise label the heuristic and uncertainty; execute and observe; retrieve versioned local or permitted external evidence only for identified gaps; then update the hypothesis/plan and verify actual effects. Preserve raw evidence provenance and scoped proposed skills separately; promote a procedure only after independent fresh-instance checks and allow refutation/expiry. Reuse `settlement_v1`, `environment_learning_v1`, existing condition store and confinement machinery rather than inventing parallel stores. This is a direction and hypothesis, not an implemented end-to-end agent. Tools should be scoped to authorized reversible actions where feasible; neither snapshot nor a textual safety label proves absence of external effects.

The earliest decisive experiment is a preregistered **source-experience → unseen environment-family transfer** comparison: fix problem distributions, resources, seeds, exclusions and success/cost metrics before running; compare empty memory, exact trace cache, shuffled/backwards memory and learned abstractions with the same observation/action budget. Include contradictory and changed-precondition cases, restart persistence, an independent verifier, and record all failures and total probe/verification cost. Freeze the implementation before truly held-out families are revealed. An improvement must beat the cache and wrong-memory controls without increasing unsafe or false-success outcomes; otherwise revise or reject the method. A single held-out success is not a general guarantee. After container-local execution and independent verification are actually established, run the previously specified Pro HARD-51, FrontierSWE v2 and Terminal-Bench 4.0 protocols; a GUI adapter and appropriately provisioned OSWorld VM are separate requirements.

Rejected as first choices: exhaustive environment enumeration (unbounded and unsafe); replay alone (cannot solve unseen cases); a single hard-coded workflow per benchmark (leakage/overfit); collecting more documents as a substitute for reasoning; pretending a source-count threshold proves completeness; and adding another runtime model (violates the constraint). A trained model may become a research candidate only if its parameter origins, mechanisms and runtime independence are justified and tested; the evidence here does not establish such a model.

## Change made in this work

A reproduction showed two failed heuristic probes leaving an integer environment at 2 instead of restoring 0. Both `right_question` and the underlying scan's `_choose_action` had the same bug. They now share `environment_learning_v1.probe_closeness`: it restores in `finally` after an attempted action, declines to act if snapshot fails, and raises if restore fails. The composed loop stops with `OPEN__PROBE_RESTORE_FAILED` before further probing/planning. Regression tests cover action-after-mutation failure, heuristic failure, snapshot failure, restoration failure and normal probes. This relies on the environment actually honoring its snapshot contract; it cannot undo real external effects or prove isolation. It is a producer-run reliability regression, not an independently verified capability gain.
