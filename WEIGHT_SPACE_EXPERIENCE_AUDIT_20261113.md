# Experience distillation: evidence and pilot readiness (2026-11-13)

## Answer and research

Adding a trainable model, persistent adapters, external memory and an independent verifier is a plausible research direction, not proof of universal computer use, learning a general rule after one example, retention forever, or sentience. Memory can support useful generalization without weight updates. Weight changes can improve a policy but can also forget, overfit, absorb poisoned data, or make a previously safe policy unsafe. Some test-time-training methods reset at the end of a task; persistent adapters remain only if saved and loaded and can still be overwritten or cause regressions. Consciousness remains a separate, unresolved scientific question.

* [Gou et al., *Sample-Efficient Learning from Agent Experience*, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.21051v1) sample a single teacher decision at a recorded history, with the teacher conditioned on collected experience and the student not conditioned on that extra context. Training on sampled teacher decisions avoids **additional environment interaction during distillation**, not the costs of collecting experience, generating teacher tokens, training, or evaluation. They report on 749 curated SWE tasks and six text games; the claimed minimum 64.8% is a fraction of the **in-context learning gain**, not task accuracy. These are their results, not this repository's. Teacher decisions can still be wrong; their paper describes examples of that. A third-party machine review [questions a sample-efficiency comparison and reports no released code/data at its review time](https://pith.science/paper/2607.21051); treat that as a review to investigate, not established disproof.
* [ASCENT v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05303v1) trains persistent LoRA fast weights online using a **frozen initial teacher** conditioned on an accepted episode as hindsight, with next-token distribution distillation; it skips rejected episodes. In its evaluated ALFWorld, WebShop and AppWorld streams, the authors report improvements and held-out-scene transfer; direct imitation or single-attempt policy gradients were unstable in their settings. A terminal acceptance signal does not prove every individual action was safe or correct. These results do not establish arbitrary GUI or coding mastery.
* [Letta's memory-model proposal](https://www.letta.com/blog/towards-agents-that-learn/) motivates training memory-writing behavior for future use; it does not establish that both memory and weight updates are necessary or sufficient for general intelligence.
* [Can Coding Agents Sustain Continual Evolution without Forgetting?](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.03219) studies two evolving coding-agent harnesses and reports substantial forgetting across its tested task transitions. This motivates retention tests for both harness and weight changes; it is not a measurement of Brain.

## What exists here, and what does not follow

* Brain contains runtime skills, memory, a model projection and receipt/verification machinery. The unified model is a compiled descriptor/index, **not trained LLM weights**. The receipt workflow can reproduce capability executions; reproduction alone does not establish semantic correctness or suitability as a learning target.
* A separate project artifact, `canonical/capabilities/opus55/S0E_STANZA_LORA_CAUSAL_ABLATION_RESULT_001.json`, records an external runner's diagnostic Stanza coreference ablation: 4/5 development cases with its 26.2M LoRA delta vs 1/5 without it (6/7 vs 3/7 pair passes). This is evidence that an existing adapter mattered on those cases, not that Brain learned its weights from agent experience or that coding transfer was demonstrated. The associated `S0E_STANZA_LORA_ABLATION_PREFLIGHT_INCIDENT_V1.json` explicitly records an earlier dependency failure, not a semantic result. Broad statements that the entire project has never used a LoRA are therefore too strong.
* The previous `verified_experience_census_20261112.py` called 170 joined records with a verdict and reproduction metadata "training-ready" (105 producer passes). That was **not** a trajectory training corpus: the label checked only metadata, not task, action/observation history, consent, independent outcome quality, replayability, or held-out separation. The census has now been corrected: it counts these as metadata claims, explicitly says training readiness and independence are not assessed, reports duplicate IDs, and no longer treats an absent model-dependency count as zero. Its old one-instance-per-capability/depth calculation was an assumption, not a comprehensive count or a statistically justified threshold. The misleading JSON fields were removed; any external consumers must migrate to the new metadata-named fields.
* New read-only audit: `python3 canonical/tools/audit_distillation_receipts_v1.py`. On this checkout, 296 linked verification records, 296 metadata-consistent reproductions, 170 Boolean verdicts, 105 producer passes, 120 empty invocation arguments, and **zero** linked receipts matching the audit's explicit `task` plus observation/action `trajectory` shape. This counts records, not distinct independent experiments; it checks metadata and hashes against each other, not cryptographic authenticity or replay. Different stores and other representations are outside its scope. A matching shape would still not mean the experience is safe or valid for training. Tests: `python -m pytest -q canonical/tests/test_audit_distillation_receipts_v1.py` (4 passed).
* In this session no GPU was detected, RAM is approximately 3.8 GiB, and torch, transformers, PEFT and local LLM checkpoint weights were not identified. These are observations about this sandbox, not a claim about all infrastructure. No model weights were trained, no experience-distillation model was run, and no fresh independent transfer gain was established.

## First useful experiment, before deployment

1. **Fix the measurement and data bottleneck first.** Select a narrow, permissioned coding environment with sandbox snapshots, hidden behavioral tests and real agent trajectories: task, observation/action history, pre/post state hashes, tool versions, outcome, independently executed tests, provenance and consent. Filter secrets and prompt injection; separate untrusted observations from instructions. Keep raw data private where needed. Do not infer successful training examples from fail-closed receipts or from hash matches.
2. **Freeze the protocol and splits before training.** Group train, validation and truly blinded test by repository, task family and near-duplicate/lineage, with a separate retention/safety suite. Record dataset and checkpoint versions. Do not inspect or repeatedly tune on test cases. Include different environments and negative/adversarial cases, not merely changed strings. Evaluate action correctness and unauthorized/destructive side effects, not just syntax or self-reported success.
3. **Use an actual licensed, pinned trainable model and enough compute.** On identical task streams and matched budgets/seeds, compare frozen no-memory; frozen plus retrieval; direct SFT/LoRA on accepted traces; one-step experience-conditioned teacher distillation; an ASCENT-inspired frozen-teacher distribution-distillation variant; and the best weight variant plus memory. Specify prompt construction, token masks and optimization beforehand; separate experience conditioned on final outcomes from the student's input. The paper methods differ; do not label a simplified SFT implementation as ASCENT. Include base/no-experience-teacher and retrieval-quality controls. Evaluation must execute real isolated tasks, not measure only loss or imitation.
4. **Measure before learning from each task.** Report paired prequential success, held-out unseen-project and family transfer, safety violations and refusal quality, learning/forgetting on earlier tasks, variance across seeds, inference tokens, teacher tokens, GPU time, wall-clock and total cost. Measure whether combined memory plus weights improves over each alone. A narrow positive result stays narrow.
5. **Quarantine updates.** Train a candidate adapter offline in isolated storage; snapshot model, adapter, optimizer, dataset and hashes. Independent checks on hidden tasks, regression and safety decide whether to promote; reject and roll back otherwise. Human approval for consequential actions, permissions enforced outside the model, and protections against training data poisoning are required. No automatic online update of a production policy based solely on its own success report.

This pilot needs a suitable model/checkpoint, accessible compute, and sufficient approved trajectory data. Until those exist, a trainer stub or synthetic receipt conversion would be easy to mistake for evidence while answering none of the real transfer questions.
